# Love Laundry Management System

> **Proprietary Software — All Rights Reserved**

A modern management system developed for **Love Laundry** to support and streamline laundry operations, customer management, order processing, invoicing, payments, reporting, and related business activities.

---

## 📋 About the Project

The **Love Laundry Management System** is a proprietary software application developed specifically for the operational and business requirements of **Love Laundry**.

The system is intended to provide an integrated platform for managing laundry-related activities, including:

* Customer management
* Laundry orders
* Invoice management
* Payment tracking
* Outstanding balances
* Laundry operations
* Linen management and tracking
* Reports and analytics
* Business administration
* Other operational features implemented within the system

The application may be continuously enhanced with new features, improvements, integrations, and operational tools.
