The **Love Laundry Management System** is a centralized software platform developed to streamline laundry operations, customer management, order processing, financial management, linen tracking, and business administration.

## Platform Overview

<p align="center">
  <img src="docs/images/dashboard.png" alt="Management Dashboard" width="90%">
</p>

## Core Features

| Feature | Description |
|---|---|
| Customer Management | Customer records and service management |
| Order Management | Laundry orders and processing workflows |
| Financial Management | Invoices, payments and outstanding balances |
| Linen Management | Linen inventory and tracking |
| Business Administration | Administrative and operational controls |
| Reporting & Analytics | Business insights and operational reports |
| Process Integration | Connected workflows and automated updates |
